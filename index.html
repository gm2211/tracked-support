Recording laps

Tap Start on the Record tab, then tap the flag as you cross start/finish. Every lap after that is timed automatically, with the screen locked too. tracked is for closed circuits: mount your phone and don't touch it while driving.

Importing sessions

tracked reads Garmin .fit, AiM .xrk and Race Studio 2 .drk and .xls files. Open them from the Files app or the share sheet, or download directly from an AiM Solo 2 or Solo 2 DL over Wi-Fi.

Your account

tracked is invite-only for now. To delete your account and its data, open Settings in the app and choose Delete account.
